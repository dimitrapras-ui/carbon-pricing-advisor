⚠️ Risk Flags & Mitigation
▼

⚠️ Shadow price may not drive operational change [Medium]

60%+ companies use shadow prices, but only 40% report material impact on decisions. Shadow prices work for CAPEX but often fail to change daily operations.

→ Mitigation: Start with shadow price for capital decisions, transition to internal fee by Year 2

Tech companies typically have 70-90% of emissions in supply chain (Scope 3). True footprint likely 3-10x larger than 50,000 tons.

→ Mitigation: Conduct Scope 3 inventory by Q3 2026, extend price coverage by 2028
